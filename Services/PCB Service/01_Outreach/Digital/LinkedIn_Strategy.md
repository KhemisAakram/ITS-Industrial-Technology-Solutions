# LinkedIn Strategy – ITS PCB Repair

> Platform: LinkedIn ONLY (Facebook dropped)
> Profile type: Personal name + ITS title
> Goal: Authority building → inbound inquiries from industrial decision-makers (Skikda + Est Algérien)

---

## 1. Why LinkedIn (Honest Positioning)

- LinkedIn has fewer users than Facebook in Algeria, but the users it has ARE the decision-makers: maintenance managers, plant directors, engineers, workshop owners.
- Almost no local PCB repair competitor is active on LinkedIn. Blue ocean.
- Strategy shifts from volume (many groups) to precision (few, right connections + authority content).
- Prices stay OFF LinkedIn. LinkedIn builds trust; WhatsApp closes the deal.

---

## 5. Posting Cadence

- 3 posts/week (Sun, Tue, Thu) — consistency beats volume
- Best time: 8:00–10:00 (engineers checking LinkedIn at work) or 17:00–19:00
- Sun–Thu business days
- Max 3 hashtags per post
- No phone numbers in posts → "Send me a DM" or "Comment 'PCB'"
- Reply to every comment within 1 hour (algorithm boost)

## 7. Engagement Rules (10 min/day)

1. Comment on 5–10 relevant posts daily (industrial pages, engineers' posts)
2. Congratulate connections on posts/new roles
3. Respond to ALL comments and DMs within 1 hour
4. Tag satisfied clients in case-study posts (their network sees you)

## 9. Inbound Conversion Flow

1. Prospect sees profile/post
2. Sends DM or connection request
3. You reply within 1 hour → "Send me a photo of the board (recto/verso) on WhatsApp: 0699 32 40 76"
4. WhatsApp scripts take over (see WhatsApp_Scripts.md)
5. Job done → ask for referral + LinkedIn recommendation

---

## 10. Loyalty Integration (LinkedIn part)

- After each successful repair + positive feedback → ask for a LinkedIn recommendation (permanent credibility)
- Every 5th repair for a client → mini case-study post, tag them
- Monthly DM to past clients: "Any boards needing attention?"
